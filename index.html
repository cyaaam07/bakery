Our story

Founded in 2015 by pastry chef Julia Simmons, Sweet Delights Bakery has grown
from a small, local shop to a beloved community landmark. Julia's dream was
to create a space where people could gather, enjoy mouth-watering baked goods,
and feel at home. Today, we continue to honor that vision with every cake, cookie,
and loaf of bread that comes out of our ovens.
